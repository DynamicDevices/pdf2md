# 7.16.3 TX Current


![Figure 7-8 and 7-9: TX Current Graphs](./images/page_29_diagram.png)


**Image Description:**

The image displays two graphs and a table detailing the transmitter (TX) current characteristics of the CC1312R7 device.

*   **Figure 7-8: TX Current vs. Temperature**
    *   **Title:** TX Current vs. Temperature
    *   **Conditions:** 50 kbps, 868.3 MHz, +10 dBm, VDDS = 3.6 V
    *   **X-axis:** Temperature in degrees Celsius (°C), ranging from -40 to 100.
    *   **Y-axis:** Current in milliamperes (mA), ranging from 12 to 18.
    *   **Data Trend:** The graph shows a positive correlation between temperature and TX current. The current consumption increases almost linearly from approximately 12.6 mA at -40°C to about 14.7 mA at 100°C.

*   **Figure 7-9: TX Current vs. Supply Voltage (VDDS)**
    *   **Title:** TX Current vs. VDDS
    *   **Conditions:** 50 kbps, 868.3 MHz, +10 dBm
    *   **X-axis:** Supply Voltage (VDDS) in Volts (V), ranging from 1.8 to 3.8.
    *   **Y-axis:** Current in milliamperes (mA), ranging from 12 to 26.
    *   **Data Trend:** The graph illustrates an inverse relationship between supply voltage and TX current. The current is highest at the lowest voltage, starting at approximately 25.5 mA at 1.8 V. It drops sharply until about 2.2 V and then continues to decrease more gradually, reaching about 13.5 mA at 3.8 V.
